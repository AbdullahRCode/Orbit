How it works

From a midnight message to a retained file.

Here is the path an inquiry takes through Orbit, and what you see along the way.

1. Capture

An inquiry arrives on Instagram, your website or email. Orbit answers within seconds, in a tone tuned to your practice, at any hour. Duplicates are merged, sources are recorded, nothing is lost.

2. Qualify

The assistant asks a few short questions: goal, timeline, current status, service of interest. It shares general information with official sources where helpful, and it never assesses eligibility or recommends a pathway. That is your work, protected by design.

3. Score and route

Each lead is scored against your intake criteria: intent, urgency, fit, service value. High scores go to the front of your attention. Poor fits are handled politely without costing you a minute.

4. Book

Qualified leads are offered your real availability and booked directly into your calendar, with reminders that cut no-shows. If a conversation needs a human before booking, it waits for one.

5. Follow up

No-shows are recovered, quiet leads are re-engaged within platform rules, and post-consultation next steps are drafted for your approval. Nothing substantive is sent without a person signing off.

6. Report

Every morning at 8am your briefing lands: new qualified leads, items waiting on approval, marketing movements, cost per lead, and revenue attributed to its source. Three minutes, whole practice.

Where the humans are

Automatic where it is safe.
Approved where it matters.

Orbit runs on three permission levels, configured with you during setup.

Level 1, automatic

Internal work

Classification, research, scoring, analytics and drafts. The system's homework, done quietly in the background.

Level 2, your approval

External messages

Follow-ups, content and outreach wait in your approval queue. One tap to send, edit or discard.

Level 3, licensed professional only

Advice and submissions

Anything touching professional judgment belongs to the licensed consultant. Orbit prepares and organizes. It never decides.
