Compliance by design

Your licence is the business.
We built around it.

Immigration consulting is a regulated profession, and most AI tools were not built with that in mind. Orbit was built for nothing else. Here is exactly how the lines are drawn.

Only licensed professionals advise. Full stop.

Under section 91 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, only authorized representatives, including licensees of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants, may provide Canadian immigration advice or representation for a fee.

Orbit's assistant never assesses eligibility, never recommends a pathway, and never predicts an outcome. It shares general information with links to official Government of Canada sources, collects facts, qualifies against your own intake criteria, and books consultations. Conversations that approach advice are escalated to you.

Aligned with the professional conduct rules you answer to

The CICC Code of Professional Conduct governs how licensees market and run their practices, and you remain responsible for the tools acting on your behalf. Orbit is designed so that responsibility is easy to carry.

Marketing produced through Orbit never guarantees application outcomes and never implies a relationship with any government. Testimonials are only ever real ones. And Orbit is priced as a flat monthly service, never per lead and never as a referral arrangement.

A person approves what matters

Orbit runs on three permission levels. Internal work such as research, classification and drafting runs automatically. External messages wait in an approval queue for a human. Professional judgment, advice and submissions belong exclusively to the licensed consultant and are never automated.

Everything on the record

Every action the system takes is written to an audit log: what happened, when, from which source, and who approved it. If you are ever asked to account for a communication, the record is one search away.

Client data treated like it deserves

Immigration files hold some of the most sensitive personal information there is. Orbit operates under Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, with data minimization, consent-based collection, and a signed data processing agreement with every practice. Each practice's data is isolated from every other practice's, always.

Honest about being an assistant

Orbit's assistant identifies itself as a digital assistant when asked and wherever disclosure is required. In a profession built on trust, we think that is not a limitation. It is the point.

Who processes data, exactly

Three infrastructure providers process data on our instructions: Supabase hosts the encrypted database and files, Anthropic provides the AI processing (API inputs are not used to train its models by default), and Resend delivers email. Data is never sold and never used for advertising, and every practice signs a data processing agreement.

Governed like serious software

Orbit's oversight model draws on recognized AI risk management practice, including the NIST AI Risk Management Framework: human oversight roles, source traceability, confidence-aware escalation, and the ability to pause any automated behavior instantly.

This page describes how Orbit is designed. It is not legal advice, and Orbit is not a law firm or an immigration consultancy.
