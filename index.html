Questions consultants ask

Straight answers.

Does Orbit give immigration advice to my leads?

No. Orbit shares general information with links to official government sources, collects facts, qualifies against your intake criteria and books consultations. Anything that approaches advice is escalated to you. The professional judgment, and the file, remain yours.

Does it replace my case management software?

No. Orbit is the layer around your existing tools. It handles what happens before someone becomes a client, and the intelligence about your practice afterward. Your case management system keeps doing its job.

Will clients know they are talking to an assistant?

Yes, when they ask and wherever disclosure is required. In a regulated profession, transparency is a feature. The assistant is fast, accurate and polite, and it hands off to a human the moment the conversation needs one.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $1,500 per month, and founding practices receive reduced setup in exchange for a documented case study. A single additional retained file typically covers the month. See the pricing page for the full breakdown.

How long does setup take?

Most practices are live within two to three weeks. We connect your channels, tune the assistant to your services and intake criteria, and run supervised conversations before anything reaches a real lead unattended.
